Tennis has been part of my life for years — on the court and as a spectator. The individual nature of the game is what I connect with most: no team to lean on, every point earned under pressure, and the mental game as important as the technical one. The ATP Tour is one of the most compelling competitions in sport, and it’s something I follow closely — from the Australian Open in my own backyard to the clay at Roland Garros.
